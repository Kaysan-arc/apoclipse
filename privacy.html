Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Apoclipse Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner. It does not offer accounts to other people and does not collect data from the public.

What the App accesses

After the owner signs in with TikTok, the App receives the owner's basic profile information (user.info.basic) and permission to upload videos (video.upload) to the owner's own account.

Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on any server and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's app settings, or by logging out in the App, which deletes the local tokens.
